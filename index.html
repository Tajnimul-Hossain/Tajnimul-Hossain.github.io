Selected Work

Project 01

Coordinated Drone Swarms

Precision Agriculture · Fisheries · Drone Shows

A three-drone coordinated swarm developed at NIRO Lab and taken from CoppeliaSim simulation through to outdoor flight tests. Custom quadcopters on Pixhawk 2.4.8 with M9N GPS and a centralized ground-control station perform autonomous takeoff, waypoint navigation, formation control, and collision avoidance, with a modular payload for spraying, fish-feed dispensing, or synchronized LED light shows.
